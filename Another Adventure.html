Design notes

  • The cheerful narrator and the slips: the narrator reframes hardship as adventure, as the parents do. Slips let reality, and later the castle, show through. Illusion vs. Reality, Deception.
  • Meters that drain no matter what: the chaos is not something the child can fix. Fear & Instability.
  • Quiet actions help most: scavenging, hiding coins and odd jobs build the jar, while the cheerful options cost you. Self-sufficiency.
  • Studying and saving to leave: the way out is through your own effort and learning. The Power of Education.
  • Food and Warmth vs. Trust: physical needs can be partly refilled, but trust mostly only wears down. Physical vs. Emotional wounds.
  • Cracking glass, darkening world, empty lunch line: the illusion breaks and the child is alone with it. Isolation, Fear & Instability.
  • Effects button: turns off flashing, scare bursts and flicker.

Another Adventure!

A game about a family that always says everything is fine.

Contains flashing, flickering and unsettling imagery. Headphones recommended. Use the Effects button (bottom right) to turn the flashing down.
